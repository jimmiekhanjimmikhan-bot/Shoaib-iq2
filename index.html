No. You can start with one focused task and add related work later.

Can I ask for multiple concepts?

Yes — the number of concepts is agreed in the proposal, so the scope and revisions stay clear.

Frequently asked questions

What should I send before getting a quote?

Tell me your service, target audience, desired outcome, deadline and any reference material or existing content.

How do project revisions work?

Revision rounds are agreed in the proposal before work starts. Feedback within the agreed scope is included; new requests are quoted separately.

Do you offer landing pages, content and chatbots?

Yes — landing pages, business websites, content writing, translation and AI chatbots, each scoped around your goal.

Can you build industrial software and dashboards?

Yes. ERP-style portals, inventory tools, machine-monitoring dashboards and compliance trackers; the workflow is discussed before scoping.

      Terms & project agreement

      1. Scope & orders

      Each project is based on the written scope agreed in chat or email. Work outside the agreed scope is quoted separately before it starts.

      2. Payment

      Payment terms are agreed in the written proposal. Work begins once the agreed payment is confirmed. Delivered files are released after payment is verified.

      3. Revisions

      Revision rounds are agreed in the proposal. Revisions must stay within the original brief; new requests are quoted separately.

      4. Ownership

      On full payment, rights to the final deliverables transfer to you. We may reference the project in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.

      5. Cancellation & refunds

      Cancellation and refund conditions are written into each proposal and depend on the work already completed.

      6. Privacy

      Your files and data are used only to deliver your project and are never sold.

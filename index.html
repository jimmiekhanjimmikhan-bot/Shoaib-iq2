      About ShoaibScale.Online

      ShoaibScale.Online — Industrial Programs is a digital studio building software, websites and brand systems for manufacturers, suppliers and industrial service companies. Scope is fixed before work starts, payment sits in escrow, and every delivery comes with a strict 3-day revision window — no surprises, no hidden fees.

      Contact us

      Tell us about your project and we’ll reply by email. Logged-in customers can also use Live Chat from the menu.

      Frequently asked questions

      How does escrow work?

      Your payment is held securely. The specialist starts once funds are confirmed, and funds release only after you approve the delivery.

      How long is the revision window?

      Strictly 3 days from delivery. The countdown on your dashboard shows exactly how long remains; after it ends the order is auto-completed.

      Which currencies can I pay in?

      Prices display in USD, PKR, EUR and AED. Final invoicing is confirmed in chat before payment.

      Do I own the final files?

      Yes. Full rights transfer to you upon payment, with source files included.

      Can I request a refund?

      If we fail to deliver to the agreed brief after revisions, escrow funds are returned in full.

      Terms & Conditions

      1. Scope & orders

      Each order is based on the package or written brief agreed in chat. Work outside the agreed scope is quoted separately before it starts.

      2. Escrow & payment

      Work begins once payment is confirmed in escrow. Funds are released to the specialist when you approve the delivery or when the revision window ends.

      3. Revisions

      Each delivery includes a strict 3-day revision window. Revisions must stay within the original brief; after the window closes the order is completed automatically.

      4. Ownership

      On full payment, rights to the final deliverables transfer to you. We may reference the project in our portfolio unless you request an NDA.

      5. Refunds

      If we cannot deliver to the agreed brief after the included revisions, escrow funds are returned in full. Refunds are not available for work already approved.

      6. Privacy

      Your files and data are used only to deliver your project and are never sold or shared with third parties.

      7. Changes

      We may update these terms; orders in progress stay under the terms they were placed with.
